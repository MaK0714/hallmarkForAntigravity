# SFMOMA | Culture and events

**Borrow the move.** Give the programme the expressive canvas. Keep ticketing, hours and access recognizable from every point in the visit flow.

### Intent and emotional register

An art-curious visitor needs to find an exhibition and decide whether a visit is practical. Prestigious work and generous image scale suggest anticipation. Explicit visit logistics make the invitation actionable. The latter is especially important for a visitor deciding around cost, mobility or a day when the museum may be closed.

### Observed decisions

| Decision | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Utility header | Menu, search, membership and tickets support different visitor goals. Desktop uses visible text for the ticket action; the sampled phone header compacts some actions into icons. Keep their accessible labels and hit areas when adapting. |
| Exhibition lead | A strong artwork or installation image sets the mood. The exhibition name and current status orient the visitor. In the phone sample, caption and title sit below the lead image, leaving the artwork room. |
| Continuing programme | Status words, titles and descriptions differentiate artwork on view, upcoming events and editorial reads. The collection functions as a programme. |
| Visit guide | Jump links group the decisions around prices, hours, arrival, things to do and discounts. A browser click on the hours link moved to the corresponding section and updated the hash. The price table keeps like items comparable. The ticket link follows release of cost information. |
| Typography and palette | The page declares SFMOMASans. Red concentrates in identity, notice and utility chrome; exhibit assets supply a second register. The sampled display heading for the featured exhibit dropped from 32px desktop to 22px phone. |

### Designer rationale, motion and tradeoffs

The museum's design studio documents the relationship of the visual identity to the city and its surroundings and explains its custom display and text type. That rationale is about the identity, with placement choices inferred from the live pages. A muted autoplay video element was present and paused in the sampled capture. Playback, reduced motion and checkout were outside the sample. A cookie banner occupied much of the mobile viewport and competed with exhibition discovery.

### Transfer

Use available artwork as the first emotional cue and verified details to follow. Give a closed date, access requirement, or sold-out status the same communication care as the event itself. Check cost and arrival information before sending a visitor to an external ticketing provider.
