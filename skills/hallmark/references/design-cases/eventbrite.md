# Eventbrite

**Borrow the move.** Keep event search and category browsing available beside a campaign.

## Task and emotional interpretation

Visitor task: discover an event. Emotional register: social anticipation. This is an interpretation of the sampled presentation, not a claim about the designers' private intent.

## Observed composition and type

The sampled home page pairs search and location with a music campaign, category icons and a local browsing section. The phone consent panel occupies a large central area.

Founders Grotesk R stack; orange utility accents and campaign photography separate brand from event content.

## Transfer and placement

Use for events and programmes with genuine location and category data. Keep dates and availability on results.

## When it fails

Fails when a promotional hero or overlays push the first useful listing too far down. Never infer local inventory from the campaign image.

## Motion adaptation

Optional campaign motion must not move search controls. Preserve a usable still image and direct search route. These are adaptation recommendations. Animation timing, reduced-motion handling and downstream transactions were not validated in this sample.

## Evidence boundaries

Sampled 19 November 2026 at 1440x900 and 375x812. This is a bounded page sample, not an accessibility certification or a claim that the entire site is exemplary. Initial overlays are retained in the evidence; some dismiss controls were exercised in a separate capture. Computed font stacks describe declarations, not proof of downloaded font identity.
