# Linear | SaaS and developer tools

**Borrow the move.** Demonstrate a single recognizable workflow with concrete data and human actions. Let the interface be the picture.

### Intent and emotional register

A product team needs to establish whether the software fits real work. The restrained hierarchy and dense product detail suggest precision and momentum. A visitor can recognize a triage or review problem before committing to signup. That is an interpretation of the homepage.

### Observed decisions

| Decision | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Global links | Product and Resources organize exploration. Pricing and Docs let an evaluator reach decision material. Login and signup serve existing and prospective users separately. |
| Opening claim and release link | The headline identifies the kind of system. A smaller release link gives returning visitors something timely without requiring a modal or duplicating the signup action. |
| Product demonstration | The example shows a reported issue, a person asking for help, a draft pull request and a visible review step. Specific objects carry the claim. Treat this as marketing demonstration; authenticated behavior was outside the sample. |
| Subsequent feature sections | Individual workflows such as intake get their own example. The audience sees inputs, intermediate states and human decisions instead of reading a pile of capability adjectives. |
| Type and surfaces | The headline declared Inter Variable at 64px desktop and 38px mobile. Compact sans labels and layered dark surfaces assign most of the contrast to the title and task. On mobile the demonstration reads as a cropped issue vignette. |

### Author-stated rationale and motion

Linear's product redesign write-up says the team reduced visual noise and adjusted hierarchy and density in navigation, headers and panels. That rationale is about the **app**. It supplies useful context for the homepage's product example. Ambient dot loops appeared in the live homepage's animation list, with a sampled 2.8-second duration. The early desktop capture showed an unpainted hero; a subsequent desktop capture with reduced motion displayed it. Dot animations were still present in that document under the reduced preference. Treat entry visibility and quiet motion as separate checks.

### Transfer

Reconstruct a short, truthful workflow using the user's genuine objects, vocabulary and constraints. Keep a still, readable version of any demo, especially on mobile. Test that the main promise is legible if the visual loads late. A nearly black palette is optional; hierarchy and authentic workflow detail are the parts worth carrying over.
