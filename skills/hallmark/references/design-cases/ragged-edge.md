# Ragged Edge | Portfolio and agency

**Borrow the move.** Put the reasoning and artifact next to each other. A project should make the studio's taste visible and show what that taste was for.

### Intent and emotional register

A buyer or collaborator wants a point of view and evidence the studio can execute it. The opening makes a provocative claim; partnership cards and story chapters let the visitor test it. The deliberate friction in the voice reads as selective and confident. That is an interpretation of the portfolio.

### Observed decisions

| Decision | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Introduction and project cards | A short position statement leads into work. Cards join a client, a concise premise and a reported outcome. Read the claims as the studio's reporting. Transfer the structure only if the project has real evidence. |
| Case study chapters | The Granola case provides anchors for overview, definition, creation, launch and impact. The sequence serves both a skimmer looking for output and a reader evaluating a decision. |
| Contact and content controls | Project links, the list of partnerships, newsletter signup and copyable contact details serve different moments. The newsletter sits after the work. |
| Type and imagery | The home navigation declares Grit-Regular. The live case hero uses ABCDiatypeExpanded-Bold and lets a long sentence occupy most of the width. Rounded, pale navigation sits in a much quieter visual register. The commissioned identity in the Granola case gives display, utility and handwriting separate jobs. Case assets show the brand in context; a confident visual cue can recur when location or medium changes. |

### Author-stated rationale

The studio describes Granola's hand-drawn mark as a reference to non-linear growth and its bespoke script as based on a co-founder's handwriting. It assigns the slab serif character and the grotesque functional product work. The authors also explain why illustration and warmth express the messiness of human work. These are stated intentions, distinct from inferred homepage effects.

### Motion and tradeoffs

A muted autoplay video element was present on the opening route. The headless first viewport remained a blank dark surface in both viewport samples. The Granola case route rendered its actual opening, with a giant cropped studio wordmark, pill navigation and wide black lettering. The phone version keeps the headline but collapses the desktop link row into a single plus-shaped menu control. Home timing, interruption and reduced-motion behavior are unverified. For an adaptation, make the project name and destination available before video or an entry effect finishes.

### Transfer

If the user asks for bold work, make a real choice that can provoke disagreement, then show its purpose with evidence. For a quieter consultancy, use the chapter structure and real proof without importing the confrontational voice.
