# NHS | Healthcare

**Borrow the move.** Make the stressful task easy to find. Separate immediate routing, routine actions and general learning into recognizable groups.

### Intent and emotional register

Visitors may be worried, short on time or helping someone else. The design projects familiarity through blue identity, legible type and plain destinations. This interpretation concerns information architecture. Medical wording and urgent routes must come from the responsible healthcare provider.

### Observed decisions

| Decision | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Search and primary categories | A prominent search field serves people with a condition, symptom or service in mind. Browsable categories help when the visitor does not know the exact term. The phone view retains search and gives less common routes a More entry. Clicking Health A to Z opened the matching directory. |
| Contextual service notice | The sampled page presented a time-sensitive notice near the top with concrete guidance. The notice sits in a distinct panel that can be recognized apart from the standard content. |
| Manage-your-health links | Short task labels tell the visitor what each link does. Personal transactions are conceptually separate from reading, which reduces the risk of assuming all content needs an account. |
| Service finder and urgent help | Finding a nearby service and getting urgent guidance are different decisions. Clear verbs, category labels and visibly grouped links keep these paths distinct. |
| Type and surfaces | The sampled page declared Frutiger W01 with Arial fallback. It uses scale and consistent heading levels for orientation. The opening copy sits on a solid blue surface over a staff photograph, which keeps text on a predictable background. |

### Stated guidance and motion

The NHS manual identifies Frutiger as its licensed brand font, specifies Arial fallback and documents heading roles. Its design system prioritizes consistent and accessible services. The sampled opening had no running CSS animation after the consent interaction. Other pages, reduced motion, personalised transactions and clinical routes were outside the checks. Favor a visible state change when a search or filter finishes. Use still presentation for essential instructions.

### Transfer

On a service homepage, put task verbs and routes before promotional language. Build a separate place for urgent, authoritative instructions and time-sensitive local updates. Keep the user's geography and healthcare concerns explicit. Use a calm type hierarchy and visible focus color without borrowing an official healthcare identity.
