# BAGGU | Commerce

**Borrow the move.** Give a collection one unmistakable visual conceit. As soon as shoppers compare products, put names, variants and price in a predictable product layout.

### Intent and emotional register

A shopper can arrive for a known bag or encounter a seasonal collection. The campaign makes an everyday object feel like a toy or accessory with personality. The collection gives the shopper concrete reasons to choose. Read as a sequence, the first screen says “look at this thing” and the next one says “here is the one you saw.” This is an interpretation of the visual structure.

### Observed decisions

| Decision | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Utility strip, wordmark and cart | Keeps logistics and cart access identifiable while the campaign changes. The mobile header contracts to menu, centered wordmark and cart count. |
| Metallic campaign image and split label | A wearable scale reference and conspicuous material color carry the joke. In the sampled phone crop, the bag is against an acid-yellow top rather than isolated on white. The campaign label is two widely separated words on a white band. |
| Collection shift | The collection pairs object-only and on-body imagery with a panel containing size, color, swatches, price and construction. Outline and filled actions distinguish details from purchase. The All control switched the collection to its comparable grid and updated the URL to a grid anchor. The strip of new arrivals provides a quicker entry into browsing. |
| Type and color | The sampled UI declares `studioPro`. Short uppercase labels feel decisive and keep out of the photography's way. Quiet backgrounds make the product variations legible; the playful color belongs to the photo and campaign label. |
| Social treatment | The repeating social strip is ambient brand voice. The shop and product details retain direct labels. |

### Motion, mobile and tradeoffs

A repeating social marquee was present in the sampled mobile DOM. Its CSS animation duration was about 44 seconds. It is secondary to shopping. A desktop details click left the collection URL unchanged in the sampled session. Treat the product link handoff as unverified. Reduced-motion behavior and checkout were outside the sample. The desktop campaign initially rendered a blurred image placeholder. When adapting the move, size the image frame in advance, retain a readable campaign link during loading and use a static linked label under reduced motion.

### Transfer

For a playful catalogue, shoot one specific product in an improbable but illuminating context. Keep variant availability, total price, returns and purchase controls explicit. Validate that the product pictured matches the product the visitor reaches. For a utility-first store, use a smaller campaign and show real products sooner.
