# Brilliant | Education

**Borrow the move.** Let the visitor see the learner doing one thing. The audience should understand what practice feels like and where they could start.

### Intent and emotional register

A prospective learner may feel rusty, curious or frustrated. A parent or teacher assesses the fit on someone else's behalf. The sampled homepage opens with a direct tutoring promise and separates the two audiences. Puzzle, code and chart imagery makes the promise concrete. The interpretation is that this shifts the first emotion from “I may be behind” to “I can try a small problem.”

### Observed decisions

| Decision | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Role-specific entry | Learner and parent/teacher buttons make the choice about the visitor's goal. Sign in lives independently in the header for returning users. |
| Sample activity cards | Tilted samples suggest a repertoire of actual tasks. The phone view shows this material above the headline; the title and action then restack vertically. Screenshot thumbnails support the claim and leave room for a proper practice flow. |
| Math and Coding choice | The homepage has paired subject controls and distinct explanations. Lower sections expose specific topics through subject selectors and expandable lists, giving evaluators a concrete curriculum to inspect. Clicking Algebra changed the selected control and showed algebra topics and a corresponding worked example on the same page. |
| Proof and progression | Identifiable certification and accreditation cues are separate from the activity examples. Use proof only when supplied and independently supportable. Practice, feedback and progress each get a different job. |
| Type and palette | The hero declares CoFo Robert at 54px desktop and 36px mobile. The site controls declare CoFo Brilliant. Warm near-white, black type and a single vivid green action create a clear hierarchy. |

### Motion and tradeoffs

The live rendering showed an array of learning previews. Animation sampling detected 700ms and 1000ms CSS timings on the page. Treat those as measurements, with exact trigger mapping, interruption and reduced-motion behavior unverified. Course signup and account-only feedback were outside the sample. Motion works best in a sample when it reveals how a hint or answer changes understanding. Keep navigation and progress controls immediately operable.

### Transfer

Replace a feature card with one meaningful choice and explanatory feedback, using real subject material. Keep the question, feedback and next action available as text. Use a little personality to make mistakes feel recoverable. Add the parent or teacher path only when it represents a distinct workflow.
