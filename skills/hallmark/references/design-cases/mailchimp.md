# Mailchimp

**Borrow the move.** Mix an editorial voice with explicit product and account routes.

## Task and emotional interpretation

Visitor task: evaluate a business product. Emotional register: approachable professionalism. This is an interpretation of the sampled presentation, not a claim about the designers' private intent.

## Observed composition and type

The sampled page uses a serif-led personalization dialog above the marketing site. Industry selection and goal checkboxes request context before browsing. A separate cookie strip further reduces phone space.

Means Web serif and Tally or Graphik sans stacks distinguish editorial messaging from utility. Yellow and dark neutrals provide brand recognition.

## Transfer and placement

Use serif/sans contrast for an established business identity. Ask optional tailoring questions only when their benefit is clear.

## When it fails

Fails when personalization blocks understanding the product. The sampled overlay is a cautionary example; do not copy it as a default entrance.

## Motion adaptation

Use feedback for an explicit choice; avoid entrance motion that competes with reading or obscures dismiss controls. These are adaptation recommendations. Animation timing, reduced-motion handling and downstream transactions were not validated in this sample.

## Evidence boundaries

Sampled 19 November 2026 at 1440x900 and 375x812. This is a bounded page sample, not an accessibility certification or a claim that the entire site is exemplary. Initial overlays are retained in the evidence; some dismiss controls were exercised in a separate capture. Computed font stacks describe declarations, not proof of downloaded font identity.
