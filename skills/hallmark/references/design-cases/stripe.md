# Stripe

**Borrow the move.** Connect an expressive brand surface to a concrete next step.

## Task and emotional interpretation

Visitor task: evaluate infrastructure. Emotional register: technical confidence. This is an interpretation of the sampled presentation, not a claim about the designers' private intent.

## Observed composition and type

The homepage combines a multicolor ribbon, a financial infrastructure heading and a prominent action. On phone the heading is shortened. The sampled action was Request an invite.

Sohne variable stack; tight sans hierarchy contrasts with the fluid colored background.

## Transfer and placement

Use expressive material when the product has enough concrete evidence to support it. Put a real workflow or implementation detail near the promise.

## When it fails

Fails when the gradient becomes the entire idea, changing backgrounds reduce contrast, or the next action promises access that is not available.

## Motion adaptation

Keep text and actions independent of the moving background. Freeze the scene under reduced motion and verify contrast over every frame. These are adaptation recommendations. Animation timing, reduced-motion handling and downstream transactions were not validated in this sample.

## Evidence boundaries

Sampled 19 November 2026 at 1440x900 and 375x812. This is a bounded page sample, not an accessibility certification or a claim that the entire site is exemplary. Initial overlays are retained in the evidence; some dismiss controls were exercised in a separate capture. Computed font stacks describe declarations, not proof of downloaded font identity.
