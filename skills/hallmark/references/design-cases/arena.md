# Are.na

**Borrow the move.** Arrange related material before asking users to act.

## Task and emotional interpretation

Visitor task: collect ideas. Emotional register: quiet curiosity. This is an interpretation of the sampled presentation, not a claim about the designers' private intent.

## Observed composition and type

The About page has a narrow text column, plain navigation and Capture, Arrange and Search steps. The phone view gives reading priority and moves utility navigation to the bottom.

Areal with Arial fallbacks; neutral text and ample white space separate reading from navigation.

## Transfer and placement

Use for research collections, archives and creative tools. Put collection controls near the material they organize.

## When it fails

Fails when restraint makes an urgent primary task hard to find. The sampled animation frame contained unresolved pale placeholder blocks; do not copy that loading state.

## Motion adaptation

Use one stable collection preview. Keep its labels visible while assets load; animate rearrangement only to explain the result. These are adaptation recommendations. Animation timing, reduced-motion handling and downstream transactions were not validated in this sample.

## Evidence boundaries

Sampled 19 November 2026 at 1440x900 and 375x812. This is a bounded page sample, not an accessibility certification or a claim that the entire site is exemplary. Initial overlays are retained in the evidence; some dismiss controls were exercised in a separate capture. Computed font stacks describe declarations, not proof of downloaded font identity.
