# Dishoom | Hospitality and local services

**Borrow the move.** Let food and setting create anticipation. Offer a short route to menu, location and booking for the visitor who is already convinced.

### Intent and emotional register

A visitor might want to imagine a meal, check what is served, find a café or book a table. The opening salutation, literary language and dark presentation evoke warmth and nostalgia. The site explains what it celebrates through specific dishes, captions and places. Those facts give the voice something to stand on.

### Observed decisions

| Decision | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Navigation and booking | The header makes Book a table visible while the story unfolds. Mobile condenses the label to Book and puts longer navigation behind a menu icon. |
| Hero invitation | The opening quote, welcome in multiple languages and audio affordance establish mood. Menu and booking verbs appear as alternate tasks in the homepage flow. The mobile first capture shows how a consent banner can cover that path. |
| Menu and food stories | Specific dishes and eating occasions build anticipation. Real menu navigation follows so a guest can assess what they would order or whether the venue works for their group. |
| Location list and founding myths | An index of cafés supports visitors with a destination in mind. Optional stories give different locations their own character. |
| Type and color | Cheltenham BT handles the sampled display. Gill Sans Nova appears in navigation and booking controls. The serif voice suggests a printed story, while the concise sans labels keep routing legible. Muted surfaces let imagery and food become the color. |

### Author-stated rationale and motion

Dishoom says it writes a founding myth for each café to guide design details. Its journal labels these stories as myths. That is licence for narrative invention when it is clearly framed as such; addresses and booking policies still come from the project. The homepage offers an audio entry. Playback, interruptions and booking submission were outside the verified sample. Keep the invitation readable with sound and motion disabled.

### Transfer

Build a small world from a true local detail, such as the machine everyone brings in for repair. A witty aside or illustrated menu caption can carry personality. Keep date, menu, arrival and booking controls plain, especially when availability varies by location.
