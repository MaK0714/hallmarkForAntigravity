# The Pudding | Editorial and media

**Borrow the move.** Start with a surprisingly ordinary question. Let the reader try a tiny version of the underlying problem, then use the evidence to change what they thought they knew.

### Intent and emotional register

The lawn-mowing article turns a mundane chore into a way to talk about coverage path planning. The opening pairs deadpan curiosity with a small mower illustration. The humor is located in the premise and the toy-like activity. The authors put limits and uncertainty back into the research discussion after drawing the reader in.

### Observed decisions

| Decision | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Headline, credits, illustration | The headline states a specific question. Credits appear before the interaction. A small absurdly literal mower establishes the register without requiring a mascot across the page. |
| Short introduction and results shortcut | The article invites the reader to try the premise and offers a route to the results. A browser click scrolled to the results area without leaving the article. The shortcut is important when the reader has less time or cannot comfortably use the activity. |
| Replay | Recorded paths make planning differences and hesitation observable. A labeled replay has a definite job and gives the reader control over timing. |
| Escalating examples and charts | A small lawn introduces the model. Later examples add size and obstacles, compare groups and identify confounders. The chart and reporting make the payoff more than a game score. |
| Type, color and mobile | The first viewport pairs Atlas Grotesk display, Tiempos Text reading copy, dark paper, light type and an accent highlight. The heading falls from 48px to 39px in the sampled phone view. Preserve readable measures between graphic beats. |

### Motion and states

The useful motion here is movement through the task and replaying a path. Document evidence establishes the replay controls; replay timing and reduced-motion behavior were outside the verified sample. Apply the lesson with pause, restart and a still final path that communicates the same result. Keep a text explanation and reachable results outside the play surface.

### Transfer

Find a real task with a small twist. For a bike shop, invite visitors to diagnose one squeak from short descriptions and show why the answer matters. Keep access to availability and pricing direct. For sensitive stories, use the interactive premise only when it clarifies a reader's decision.
